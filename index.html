Infinitum Channel

Satisfying physics & math visuals, rendered without end.

Short vertical simulations of pendulums, fluid-like motion, particle systems, fractals, attractors and geometric patterns — paired with soft ASMR-style sound. Every clip is procedurally generated, so no two renders are the same.

What Infinitum is

Infinitum is an automated educational-entertainment channel that publishes short vertical videos of physics simulations and mathematical visualizations. Each video is procedurally generated by our own rendering pipeline, intended for relaxing, oddly satisfying viewing. All content is original, non-violent, and suitable for a general audience.

Content

Satisfying / oddly satisfying visuals Physics simulations & math art Relaxing, ASMR-style audio Short-form vertical video · ~60s · 1080×1920 Educational entertainment

How we use the TikTok API

We operate an automated publishing pipeline that renders original Infinitum videos and uploads them to our official TikTok account through the Content Posting API. The API is used solely to post our own content — there is no scraping of other accounts and no posting on behalf of other creators.
