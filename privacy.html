Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Infinitum ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Infinitum website and our automated publishing pipeline, including our use of the TikTok API.

1. Who we are

Infinitum operates an automated channel that renders and publishes original short-form videos to our own accounts on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook Reels. This website exists to describe that service and to host our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

From this website

This website does not use tracking cookies, and does not ask visitors to create an account or submit personal information. If you email us, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

From the TikTok API

Our publishing pipeline authenticates with our own official TikTok account using the TikTok API. Through this connection we access:

  • user.info.basic — basic profile information (open ID, avatar, display name) for our own official account, used only to confirm the pipeline is authenticated correctly; and
  • video.upload — used to upload Infinitum videos to our own account as drafts or posts.

We do not use the TikTok API to collect information about other TikTok users, viewers, or accounts.

3. How we use information

Information obtained through the TikTok API is used exclusively to operate our own publishing pipeline — authenticating our account and posting our own videos. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with third parties except service providers (such as cloud hosting) that are necessary to run the pipeline, under obligations to protect it.

4. Data storage and retention

Authentication tokens for our official TikTok account are stored securely and only for as long as needed to operate the publishing pipeline. We retain this information only as long as the Infinitum channel remains active, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

5. Children's privacy

This website and pipeline are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

6. Your rights

If you have questions about information we hold in connection with this website or our TikTok integration, you can contact us using the details below and we will respond to reasonable requests.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@infinitum.app.
